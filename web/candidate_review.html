Candidate Record Review

Compare a candidate's CV with the vacancy's approved, job-related requirements. You get a record of the documented evidence, with CV line references, for a recruiter to review. It never rejects, ranks or hires anyone, and it never guesses personal characteristics.

Runs entirely in this browser tab. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

Requirements

Match terms are the exact words to look for, separated by commas. Leave them empty when a person has to assess the requirement.

Existing records
