Candidate Record Review

Compare a candidate's CV with the vacancy's approved, job-related requirements. You get a record of the documented evidence, with CV line references, for a recruiter to review. It never rejects, ranks or hires anyone, and it never guesses personal characteristics.

Runs entirely in this browser tab. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere.

Input

Upload files

Start here, or type into the form below. Files are read inside this browser tab and are not sent to any server.

Requirements CSV format

One requirement per row. Only text is required. required accepts yes or no and defaults to yes. Separate match terms with semicolons.

requirement_id,text,required,match_terms
REQ-01,Hands-on experience with Python,yes,Python
REQ-02,Experience with SQL databases,yes,SQL;PostgreSQL
REQ-03,Project management certification,no,PMP;Project Management Professional
Requirements

Match terms are the exact words to look for, separated by commas. Leave them empty when a person has to assess the requirement.

Existing records

Optional. Records already on file, checked for duplicates. Nothing is merged.
