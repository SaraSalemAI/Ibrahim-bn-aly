## Digital Sustainability Research Platform

Companion platform for the paper *The Transition to Digital Accounting and Financial Reporting and the Institutionalisation of Green Accounting for Digital Sustainability in Egypt in Light of Egypt Vision 2030* (Alexandria University, Faculty of Business, 2026).

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [`platform/index.html`](platform/index.html) | Landing page linking everything below |
| [`platform/research-hub.html`](platform/research-hub.html) | Research Hub: the paper as an interactive bilingual guide |
| [`platform/green-ledger.html`](platform/green-ledger.html) | Green Ledger Workbench: applies the paper's four conditions to a company's own data |
| [`platform/Green-Ledger-Platform.html`](platform/Green-Ledger-Platform.html) | The whole Workbench as one offline HTML file (fonts, slides and QR library inside) |
| [`platform/docs/`](platform/docs) | Full paper (.docx), conference presentations (.pptx), A0 research poster and FRA policy brief (.pdf) |

`green-ledger.src.html` is the editable source of the Workbench (also published as a claude.ai artifact); `green-ledger.html` is the same page wrapped as a standalone HTML document.

To put the platform online, enable GitHub Pages for this repository (Settings → Pages → deploy from branch) and open `/platform/`.
