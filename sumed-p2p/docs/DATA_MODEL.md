The engine adds derived values prefixed with `_` (never written back to source): `_paid`, `_out` (outstanding = total − executed/reconciled payments unless the source provides it), `_outBase` (revalued at the latest FX rate on/before the as-of date), `_dpd` (days past due), `_fx`, `_totalBase`, `_debitBalance`, plus `_src` (lineage), `_issues` (parse problems), `_edits` (in-app changes, also in the audit trail) and `_derived` (explanations, e.g. supplier matched by name).
