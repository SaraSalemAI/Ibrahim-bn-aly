Chapter 02

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol — an open standard by Anthropic (Nov 2024) that connects AI assistants to the systems where data lives.
Like a USB-C port for AI — one plug, many devices
Databases, business tools, files & financial data
Open & widely adopted — thousands of servers
DatabasesSQL & warehouses
Business AppsCRM, docs, email
Market DataPrices & filings
FilesLocal & cloud
