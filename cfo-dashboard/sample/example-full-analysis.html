Red flags and recommendations

High

Receivables growing much faster than sales

Evidence
Receivables 37.4% vs revenue 5.2%; DSO 82 days
Recommended action
Age the receivables, provision for doubtful accounts, stop credit to overdue customers, and consider factoring.
High

Dividends paid while free cash flow is negative

Evidence
Free cash flow (4,712); dividends 2,888
Recommended action
Link dividends to free cash flow; pause or cut the payout until FCF is positive. Dividends are currently funded by debt or cash reserves.
Medium

Thin cover for interest payments

Evidence
Interest coverage 1.69× (comfort level ≥ 3.00×); interest expense 2,800
Recommended action
Stop adding debt, repay or reprice the most expensive facilities, and check covenant headroom with lenders.
Medium

High debt load

Evidence
Debt/equity 0.95×; net debt/EBITDA 3.33×
Recommended action
Set a deleveraging target (e.g. net debt/EBITDA below 2.5×) funded from free cash flow, asset sales or new equity.
Medium

Profits are not turning into cash

Evidence
2024: CFO 388 vs net income 1,488; 2023: CFO 2,237 vs net income 3,937
Recommended action
Review revenue recognition and working-capital build-up; tighten credit terms and collections.
Medium

Inventory building up

Evidence
Inventory 25.8% vs cost of sales 10.3%; DIO 93 days
Recommended action
Identify slow-moving and obsolete stock, test for write-downs, and align purchasing with demand.
Medium

Margin compression

Evidence
Gross margin 38.0% → 35.0%; net margin 6.8% → 2.4%
Recommended action
Break down the change into price, volume, mix and input cost; review pricing and supplier contracts.
Medium

Dividends exceed earnings

Evidence
Payout ratio 194.1%
Recommended action
Adopt a payout policy tied to sustainable earnings (for example 40–60% of net income).
Medium

Cash balance falling fast

Evidence
Cash -50.0% to 2,100; free cash flow (4,712)

Set a minimum cash buffer, defer non-essential capex, and secure committed credit lines now.
Medium

Borrowing growing faster than the business

Evidence
Total debt 26.2% vs revenue 5.2%
Recommended action
Check what the new debt funded (working capital, capex, dividends) and whether it earns more than its cost.

Assumptions and limits

  • Status colours use general rules of thumb, not your industry's benchmarks. Margins and turnover vary widely by sector.
  • Turnover, ROA and ROE use the average of opening and closing balances when the prior year is present.
  • Expenses are treated as positive costs whatever sign the file uses.
  • Total debt = short-term + long-term borrowings; leases are included only if the file reports them inside borrowings.
  • Altman Z'' is the non-manufacturing / emerging-market version (book equity).
  • Missing subtotals are computed from accounting identities and marked “derived”.

الافتراضات والحدود

  • ألوان الحالة مبنية على قواعد عامة وليست معايير قطاعك. الهوامش ومعدلات الدوران تختلف كثيراً بين القطاعات.
  • معدلات الدوران والعائد على الأصول وحقوق الملكية تستخدم متوسط رصيد أول وآخر المدة عند توفر السنة السابقة.
  • تُعامل المصروفات كتكاليف موجبة أياً كانت إشارتها في الملف.
  • إجمالي الديون = القروض قصيرة الأجل + طويلة الأجل؛ عقود الإيجار تُحتسب فقط إن ظهرت ضمن القروض.
  • مؤشر ألتمان Z'' هو نسخة الشركات غير الصناعية / الأسواق الناشئة (حقوق الملكية الدفترية).
  • الإجماليات الناقصة تُحسب من المعادلات المحاسبية وتُعلَّم بـ «محسوب».

الخطوات التالية

  3. حوّل التوصيات عالية الخطورة إلى مسؤولين ومواعيد تنفيذ.
